## Fault as described by customer
Clicking sound when turning, pulls slightly left, started a few days ago,
worse on sharp turns.

## Diagnosis
Worn CV joint, driver's side. Confirmed by clicking noise specifically on
full-lock turns, absent at straight-line speed — the key discriminator versus
a wheel bearing, which tends to produce speed-related rather than turn-related
noise.

## Fix
Replaced CV joint assembly. Repacked with new grease boot.

## Notes
Customer confirmed noise fully resolved on follow-up call one week later.
